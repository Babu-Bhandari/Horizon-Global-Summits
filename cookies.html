Cookies

Cookie policy.

Last updated 25 November 2026.

This notice describes what Horizon Global Summits stores on your device when you use this website, and how you can change it.

Cookie preference

When you choose Accept or Decline, the choice is saved in local storage in this browser under the name hohorizon-cookie. The value is either accept or decline. It is not sent to us as a form. It tells this site whether analytics may load on later visits.

Decline still saves the choice, so the notice does not keep returning, and analytics stays off.

Appearance

The light or dark theme is saved separately in local storage under the name hohorizon-theme. That preference is only used to display the site. It is not used for advertising.

Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you accept and only when a measurement ID has been added to the site. Until that ID exists, accepting does not send anything to Google. If analytics is running, Google may set its own cookies to recognise a browser across pages. You can stop that by choosing Decline.

Fonts and images

Pages request fonts from Google Fonts and some photographs from Unsplash. Those requests can include your IP address. They are needed to display the page and are not used by us to build a profile.

How to change your choice

Open Manage Cookies and choose Accept or Decline. You can also remove the saved preference by clearing this site’s data in your browser. The notice appears again when no choice is stored.

More about enquiries and contact details is in the Privacy policy.
