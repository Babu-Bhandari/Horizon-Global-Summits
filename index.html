About us

Horizon Global Summits is a UAE-based international B2B conference and executive summit organisation dedicated to bringing together business leaders, senior executives, industry experts and key decision-makers from across the globe.

Through thoughtfully curated international conferences, executive leadership forums and industry-focused summits, we create distinguished platforms for meaningful dialogue, knowledge exchange, strategic networking and impactful business partnerships.

Driven by a global vision and an unwavering commitment to excellence, we strive to bridge industries, foster cross-border collaboration and unlock new opportunities for business growth. By connecting influential minds and organisations, Horizon Global Summits aims to transform meaningful conversations into strategic alliances, sustainable opportunities and lasting global impact.

We are an approved CPD provider

At Horizon Global Summits, we know your time is valuable, and we want you to get the most out of every event you attend. From learning new industry insights to hearing from experienced professionals, our conferences are designed to give you something valuable to take back.

And there's more! As we are a CDP-accredited event organiser, you can also earn Career Development Points (CDPs) by attending our events, making your time with us even more rewarding.
