What Accept does

Accept stores “accept” in local storage under the name hohorizon-cookie. If a Google Analytics measurement ID has been configured, analytics may then load. Until that ID is added, accepting does not send data to Google.

What Decline does

Decline stores “decline” in the same place and keeps analytics off. The cookie notice stays dismissed on this browser until you choose again.
