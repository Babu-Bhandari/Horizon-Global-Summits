Our mission

Make the cross-border conversation easier to have.

Markets, policy and operating reality rarely sit in the same meeting. The forum is a place where they can.

Clarity

Sessions have a question. If a subject cannot be asked in one sentence, it does not go on the programme.

Proportion

The guest list stays small enough that a discussion can finish. Scale is not the measure.

Discretion

A private meeting is private. The public programme is the part that can be written down.

Range

Finance, trade, technology, risk and leadership share one day, because the decisions touch all five.

Hospitality

The setting should feel calm. It should never outrank the reason people travelled.

Follow-through

An introduction is only useful if both people know why it was made. The host says so.
