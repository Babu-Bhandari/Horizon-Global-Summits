Privacy

What this website stores.

This policy describes this website as it works today. It is not a substitute for advice from counsel.

Cookie preference

If you accept or decline the notice, the choice is saved in local storage on your device under the key hohorizon-cookie. Declining stores that choice and does not load analytics.

Analytics

Google Analytics is not loaded unless two things are true: you accepted the notice, and a measurement ID has been added to the data-ga attribute on the page. Until that ID is added, nothing is sent to Google.

Forms

Invitation, partnership, contact and newsletter forms are not stored on a server by this site. If an email address is set in data-email, the form opens a draft in your own mail application. If it is empty, the form tells you the note was not delivered.

A hidden field is used to ignore automated submissions. Do not fill it.

Images and type

Photographs are loaded from Unsplash. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Those services receive the ordinary technical request your browser makes.
