Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated 25 November 2026.

This policy explains what Horizon Global Summits collects through this website and how that information is used. It covers the public site only.

Who we are

Horizon Global Summits is based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For privacy questions, email connect@horizonglobalsummits.com. Business hours are 09:30 AM to 5:30 PM GST (UTC+4).

Information you give us

If you use a form, we receive the details you type. Depending on the form, that can include your name, company or organisation, email address, contact number, the kind of enquiry, and your message. Invitation requests may also include your job title, country, and the gathering or industry you are asking about.

We use those details to read your request, reply, and keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell personal information and we do not use enquiry forms for an unrelated mailing list.

A hidden field on each form is there to ignore automated submissions. Please leave it empty.

Preferences on your device

If you accept or decline the cookie notice, that choice is saved in local storage on your device under the name hohorizon-cookie. It stays in this browser until you change it or clear the site data. Declining keeps analytics off.

If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in local storage under the name hohorizon-theme so the site can open in the same appearance next time.

Analytics

Google Analytics is not loaded unless you have accepted the cookie notice and a measurement ID has been configured for the site. Until that ID is added, nothing is sent to Google Analytics. If both are in place, Google may receive technical information such as pages viewed and a general location derived from your connection. You can refuse this by choosing Decline on the Manage Cookies page.

Fonts and images

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Some photographs are loaded from Unsplash until they are replaced with our own images. Those services receive the ordinary technical request your browser makes, which can include your IP address.

How long we keep enquiries

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as we need it to respond, plan a gathering, or meet a legal or accounting duty. You can ask us what we still hold from an email you sent, and you can ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it.

Your choices

You can change the cookie choice at any time on the Manage Cookies page. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings. To ask about an enquiry you sent, email connect@horizonglobalsummits.com.
